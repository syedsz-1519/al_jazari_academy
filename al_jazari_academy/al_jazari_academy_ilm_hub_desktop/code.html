Aqsa al-Halq (Furthest / Lowest Throat)

Near Vocal Cords

Originated at the base of the throat closest to the chest. Emits the Hamzah (ء) with light glottal stop and Haa (هـ) with free-flowing soft breath without strain.

Scholarly rule: Idh-har Halqi (Clear articulation when followed by Noon Sakinah) Makhraj #1 of 17
