Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding admissions, teacher allocation, class flexibility, and fees.

Anyone with a desire to learn the Quran correctly! We welcome school and college students, working professionals, homemakers, and retirees across India and 14+ countries globally. Every new admission undergoes an initial diagnostic assessment so lessons match your exact starting aptitude.
Absolutely no age limit. We teach children as young as 6 years old with our patient child-friendly phonetics curriculum, as well as adults in their 50s and 60s memorising for the very first time. Memorising the Holy Quran is a blessed lifelong quest that honors steady consistency over prior speed.
Yes, 100% of our core classes are strictly 1-on-1. A dedicated scholar or Ustadhah works exclusively with you on private video, ensuring real-time Makhraj correction, personal pacing, and uninterrupted recitation feedback. There are never other students waiting in your session room.
Sessions run via private Google Meet or Zoom links with 1-click calendar sync. Our teachers use interactive digital Mushaf tools to highlight tajweed rules directly on your screen, with live phonetic audio replay and session summary markups sent right after every class.
Yes, over 65% of our adult learners are working professionals. We offer early dawn slots (6:00 AM – 8:30 AM IST) and late evening slots (7:00 PM – 11:30 PM IST), as well as dedicated weekend options so your recitation and revision easily integrate into everyday schedules.
Yes. Upon successful examination, students receive an official Al-Jazari Academy completion diploma with a verifiable QR serial code. Advanced students reciting the complete Quran by heart under our Sanad-certified scholars undergo formal oral examination to receive an authentic Sanad linking directly to the Prophet ﷺ.
Pace depends on your weekly commitment. At 1 page per day (Standard or Intensive), students typically complete the full 30 Juz in 2 to 2.5 years. At a gentler pace (half page per day), it spans 3 to 4 years. We emphasize solid retention over rushing through new pages.
Yes. We start from the foundational Arabic letters, articulation points (Makharij), and short vowels (Harakat) through our structured Nazira & Noorani Qaida module before progressing to longer verses or memorisation. Our mentors ensure you master correct phonetics from day one.
We offer flexible makeup classes. As long as you notify your academic coordinator at least 4 hours before class, your session credit is banked and can be rescheduled within the same calendar month. We understand that travel and family emergencies happen.
We accept UPI, Net Banking, and Debit/Credit cards in India, and Stripe/PayPal for international students. Fees are billed on a flexible month-to-month basis with zero lock-in; you can pause your enrollment or cancel anytime with zero cancellation penalties.
