Chapter 01 DPDPA 2023 • GDPR Art. 8

1. Collection of Student & Minor Information

Al-Jazari Educational Academy gathers only strictly necessary personal identifiable information (PII) required to deliver personalized Quranic instruction, manage schedule coordination, and process tuition safely. We adhere strictly to the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

family_restroom Parental Consent Protocol

For students under 18 years of age, verified explicit parental or legal guardian consent is obtained during enrollment. Parents retain full unilateral oversight over class schedules, progress dashboards, and contact records.

no_encryption_gmailerrorred Zero Data Commercialization

We categorically affirm: We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your child's personal records or learning metrics to third-party advertisers, marketing syndicates, or external data brokers.

Chapter 02 1-on-1 Secure Rooms

2. Virtual Classroom & Live Video Protocols

All interactive recitations, Tajweed evaluations, and memorization circles are conducted via enterprise, encrypted Google Meet conduits directly coordinated by academy systems.

videocam_off Strict Prohibition on Unauthorized Recordings

  • Neither tutors nor students are authorized to create local screen recordings, audio snapshots, or excerpts for social media or unauthorized distribution without mutual written endorsement.
  • Academic session recording is strictly disabled by default. If a parent requests an archived lesson for student revision, it is recorded to our encrypted cloud silo and auto-purged after 14 days.
  • Tutors operate from verified, solitary private teaching rooms ensuring zero eavesdropping or background family visibility.

Chapter 05 Academic Discipline

5. Attendance, Tardiness & Makeup Sessions

Consistency is the cornerstone of lifelong Hifz retention. We request mutual reverence for the instructor's allocated scholarly time.

Rescheduling Protocol

Notice must be submitted via the Student Portal or Academy WhatsApp minimum 4 hours in advance. Makeup slots will be provided within the same or subsequent calendar week.

Unannounced Absences

Tutors remain inside the virtual portal for 15 minutes. Failure to attend without timely notice forfeits the designated session without automatic credit transfer, barring emergencies.
