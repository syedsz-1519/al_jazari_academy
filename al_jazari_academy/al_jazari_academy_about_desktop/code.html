Our Origins & Heritage

Preserving the Sacred Oral Tradition in a Digital World

Founded in Bengaluru, India, Al-Jazari Educational Academy emerged from a pressing contemporary challenge: while modern life has accelerated, access to rigorous, authentic Quranic teachers has become increasingly fragmented. Many working professionals, university scholars, and homeschooling families yearned for classical pedagogical discipline without geographical limits.

Rather than relying on pre-recorded software or crowded virtual halls, we revived the foundational methodology of the Islamic tradition: uninterrupted, personal talaqqī (direct oral reception) paired with musyāfahah (observing vocal mouth articulation). Every letter, pause, and breath rule is monitored live by authorized reciters.

Why "Al-Jazari"?

Imam Ibn al-Jazari (751–833 AH)

Our academy is dedicated to the legacy of Shams al-Din Abu al-Khayr Muhammad ibn al-Jazari, the unparalleled luminary of Tajweed and Qira’at science. His seminal poetic treatise, Al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah, revolutionized the phonetic taxonomy of Arabic articulation points (Makharīj al-Hurūf) and phonetic attributes (Sifāt). That exact standard of precision governs our syllabus today.
