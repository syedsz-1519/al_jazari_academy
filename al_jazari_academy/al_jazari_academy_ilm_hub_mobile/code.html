Makhraj Anatomical Guide

Throat articulation points (Al-Halq)

Aqsa al-Halq (أَقْصَى الحَلْق) Deepest throat near vocal cords
ء ، هـ

Produced from the farthest part of the throat near the chest. Ensure the Hamzah is light and crisp without excessive tension.

Phonetic accuracy: 100%
